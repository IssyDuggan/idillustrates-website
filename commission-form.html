Booking & payment

I’ll review your request and confirm the final price and timing before you book. A 50% deposit secures the commission; the balance is due when the artwork is complete. Usual turnaround can be around 2 to 3 weeks.

Revisions

One round of minor revisions is included. Significant changes to the composition or further amendments may be charged separately.

Delivery

Tracked UK delivery is included. International delivery is quoted separately.

Copyright & usage

The original artwork is yours; copyright remains with the artist. Personal use is included. Reproduction, wedding stationery or commercial use requires separate permission/licensing.

Handmade artwork

Each piece is individually hand drawn and signed. Natural variations are part of the character of original artwork.
