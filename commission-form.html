Before you submit

COMMISSION
TERMS.

Payment

A 50% deposit is required before I begin your commission. The remaining 50% is due once you have received your finished artwork. I will send you a second payment request for the remaining balance.

Revisions

Your commission includes one round of revisions. I’ll do my best to make sure you’re happy with your piece, but any further amendments after your included revision will be charged at my hourly rate.

Turnaround & Deadlines

My usual turnaround time is 2–3 weeks. If you have a particular date you need your commission by, please include this in the form. I’ll always do my best to meet your deadline, and priority commissions are available for an additional fee if you need your artwork sooner.

Reference Photos

Please provide one clear, front-on photo showing the whole building or scene, along with close-ups of any details you’d particularly like me to include. Your finished illustration will be based on the reference photos you provide, so the clearer the photos, the better.

Postage & Delivery

All commissions are carefully packaged with protective backing and sent via tracked UK First Class postage. Your artwork will be posted to the delivery address you provide in this form, so please double-check that all of your details are correct before submitting. Once your commission has been posted, I’ll send you the tracking details so you can keep an eye on its journey to you.

For international postage, please leave me a note in the final comments section of the form. I’ll be in touch to discuss postage options and any additional costs.

Copyright & Usage

Your commission is for personal use only unless we have agreed otherwise in writing. Purchasing the original artwork does not transfer the copyright of the illustration to you; I retain the copyright to my artwork.

Please do not reproduce, sell, alter or use the artwork for commercial purposes without my permission. If you’d like to use your illustration for something else, such as wedding invitations, stationery, prints or business use, please get in touch. I’m always happy to chat about different options.

I may also share photographs or scans of the finished artwork on my website, social media and portfolio. If your commission is a surprise or gift and you’d prefer me to wait until a certain date before sharing it, just let me know in the final comments.

Handmade Artwork

Each commission is individually hand drawn, original artwork and signed by me. Because every piece is created by hand, small variations and the natural character of handmade work are all part of what makes your commission unique.

Your details and uploaded reference files will be sent with your enquiry to info@idillustrates.com. Please avoid uploading anything you don’t need me to see for the commission.
